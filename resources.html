Professional Context

Perhaps because the educational infrastructure is less developed than in other LIS fields like archives or libraries, there is a very robust network of professional networks that audiovisual archivists can rely on for professional resources, including workshops, resource-shares, and opportunities for community more generally.

Two of the biggest professional associations include AMIA (the Association of Moving Image Archivists) and ARSC (the Association for Recorded Sound Collections). AMIA publishes The Moving Image journal. In the international A/V archives space, there is FIAF (the International Federation of Film Archives) which publishes the Journal of Film Preservation.</p>
